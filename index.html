A few promises

Fair play

No pay-to-win. Leaderboard times are checked by the server's own clock, so records are real.

Your data stays yours

Play and browse without an account. Your settings and history stay on your phone unless you choose to go online.

Easy on ads

At most a small banner, never in the middle of the fun, and one purchase removes it for good.
