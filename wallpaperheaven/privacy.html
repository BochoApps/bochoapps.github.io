Wallpaper Heaven Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Wallpaper Heaven is an app by BochoApps ("we"). It has no account system and no servers of ours: we don't receive or store any information about you. This policy explains what the app keeps on your phone, which services it connects to, and how the banner ad in the Google Play version works.

What stays on your phone

  • Settings, favorites, history and saved searches are stored privately inside the app and are not included in Android's cloud backups. Favorites hold only image links and preview addresses.
  • Downloaded wallpapers are saved to Pictures/Wallpaper Heaven so they show in your gallery. Images the app downloads by itself for automatic changes go to a hidden cache folder in the same place, limited to the number you choose in Settings.
  • Your own photo folders, if you add them as a source, are accessed only through Android's folder picker and only the folders you pick. Photos are read on your phone to set them as wallpaper and never uploaded.
  • Backups of favorites are written only to the file you choose when you export them.

Services the app connects to

To search and download images, the app connects directly to these services. Like any website, they receive your IP address and the requests the app makes (such as your search terms and filters), under their own privacy policies:

  • Wallhaven (wallhaven.cc)
  • Wikimedia Commons; as Wikimedia asks, requests include the app's name and version
  • NASA's image library (images.nasa.gov)

All connections use HTTPS. The app follows your network setting: by default it only downloads on Wi-Fi.

Ads and the Remove ads purchase

The Google Play version is free and shows a banner ad above the app's tabs, never while you view or adjust a wallpaper full screen. The ads come from Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent ad fraud, AdMob collects information from your phone, such as its advertising ID, your IP address (which gives an approximate location) and how you interact with ads. Google explains how it uses this in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. If you're in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ads → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android's settings. We don't receive this information ourselves, only totals in AdMob's reports. Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

Remove ads is a one-time purchase through Google Play that turns the ads off for good. Google Play handles the payment, so we never see your payment details. The app asks Google Play whether your Google account owns the purchase and remembers the answer on your phone; it isn't sent to us. Once ads are removed, the app doesn't start the ad SDK at all.

Versions of the app distributed outside Google Play have no ads and no purchase.

Wallhaven API key (optional)

If you enter a Wallhaven API key, it is encrypted on your phone with a key held by Android's secure key store, sent only to Wallhaven with your searches, and never shown again or sent to us. Remove saved API key in Settings deletes it immediately.

What we don't do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics trackers, and no ads other than the banner described above.
  • We don't sell your information, and apart from the ads described above, nothing is shared with anyone for their own purposes.
  • No access to your contacts, location or camera. The app's own permissions are internet access, checking whether you're on Wi-Fi, setting the wallpaper, and starting automatic changes again after the phone restarts. In the Google Play version, the ad and purchase libraries add access to the advertising ID and to Google Play's billing.

Content

Wallpapers come from the services above, not from us. The Google Play version only shows images Wallhaven marks as safe for work. Each image links to its source page, where it can be reported.

Deleting your data

Everything the app keeps is on your phone. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android's settings, deletes your settings, favorites, history and API key. A Remove ads purchase stays with your Google account and comes back when you reinstall. Wallpapers in Pictures/Wallpaper Heaven stay until you delete them, for example with your gallery or file manager.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
