Wallpaper Heaven

Find a wallpaper you love, see it exactly as it will look on your phone, and let the app change it for you.

Find wallpapers

  • Search Wallhaven, Wikimedia Commons (freely licensed images) or NASA's image library.
  • Filter by topic, category, color and screen shape; every result is shown at your phone's shape.
  • Save favorites and downloads, and see the author and license of every Commons and NASA image.

Set it your way

  • Crop, zoom, dim or blur before setting, on the home screen, lock screen or both.
  • Automatic changes on a schedule, from your searches, favorites, downloads or your own photo folders, with a different wallpaper on each screen if you like.
  • A home-screen widget and a Quick Settings tile to change it with one tap.

Free and simple

Free, with just a small banner above the tabs, never while you view or adjust a wallpaper. Remove it for good with a one-time purchase. No account needed, and your settings, favorites and history stay on your phone.

Languages

English, Spanish, German, French, Portuguese, Japanese, Korean, Simplified Chinese and Arabic.
