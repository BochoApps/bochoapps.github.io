Delete your TouchIT account

TouchIT, by BochoApps, lets you delete your online account and all its data whenever you want.

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open TouchIT and sign in with Google if you aren't already.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete online account and confirm.

By email (if you no longer have the app)

Email support@bochoapps.com from the Google account you used to sign in, with the subject "Delete my TouchIT account". Include your nickname with its tag (for example Name#1234) if you know it. We'll delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What gets deleted

  • Your sign-in account and the details Google shared (email address, name, account identifier)
  • Your nickname, tag and country
  • All your game times and leaderboard positions
  • Your friend code and friend list (you're also removed from your friends' lists)

Nothing is kept, except that deleted data may remain in our providers' encrypted backups for a short time until those backups are replaced. Settings and your best time stored only on your phone are removed when you uninstall the app.
