TouchIT

Put your finger on the button and don't let go. The longer you hold on, the better.

How to play

  • Press and hold the button. The clock counts every millisecond.
  • Lift your finger or slide off the button and you're out.
  • The button is fingertip-sized on purpose: it takes a real finger to keep it pressed.

Play together

On one phone: up to 8 players. The screen splits into one colored area per player, the clock starts when everyone is holding, and the last finger standing wins.

Online: create a room and share its link. Up to 8 players join from their own phones, each holds their own button, and everyone sees each other's times live. The round starts when all of you are holding, and every time counts for the multiplayer leaderboard.

Online leaderboard

Sign in with Google to post your times and compare them with the world, your country and your friends, all-time or this week. Times are checked by the server's own clock, so nobody can just claim a record.

Languages

English, Spanish, German, French, Portuguese, Japanese, Korean, Simplified Chinese and Arabic.
