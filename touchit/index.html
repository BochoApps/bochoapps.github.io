TouchIT

Put your finger on the button and don't let go. The longer you hold on, the better.

How to play

  • Press and hold the button. The clock counts every millisecond.
  • Lift your finger or slide off the button and you're out.
  • The button is fingertip-sized on purpose: it takes a real finger to keep it pressed.

Play together

On one phone: up to 8 players. The screen splits into one colored area per player, the clock starts when everyone is holding, and the last finger standing wins.

Online: tap Quick match to play with anyone in an open room, or create a private room and share its link. Up to 8 players join from their own phones, each holds their own button, and everyone sees each other's times live. The round starts when all of you are holding, or after a 10-second countdown once most of you are. Every time counts for the multiplayer leaderboard, players who sit out 2 rounds in a row leave the room, and each room keeps score of who wins.

Three ways to hold on

Classic, a button that slowly drifts around the screen, or one that drifts while it slowly shrinks, each with its own leaderboard. Earn achievements to unlock 25 button styles, from Ocean to a spinning Galaxy, 15 accessories for your button, from a top hat to orbiting stars and googly eyes, and 9 shapes around it, from petals to wiggling tentacles. Play during Halloween, Christmas, Valentine's Day or spring to earn that season's looks and keep them forever.

Daily challenge

Every day there's a new target time, the same for everyone. You get 3 tries to let go as close to it as you can, and the clock hides after 3 seconds, so you have to feel the time. The closest players top the daily leaderboard.

Stats and achievements

Track your games, total time held, days in a row and weekly bests, and earn 46 achievements (some of them hidden), from your first 10 seconds to winning an online round against 7 others.

Friends and streaks

Challenge a friend to beat your best with a link, see your friends' new bests and achievements, and keep your streak of days in a row going, with one missed day forgiven each week. An optional daily reminder and a Monday recap keep you in the game.

Online leaderboard

Sign in with Google to post your times and compare them with the world, your country and your friends, all-time or this week. Times are checked by the server's own clock, so nobody can just claim a record.

Languages

English, Spanish, German, French, Portuguese, Japanese, Korean, Simplified Chinese and Arabic.
