TouchIT Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

TouchIT is a game by BochoApps ("we"). This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and how you can delete it. You can play TouchIT without an account; the online features (leaderboards and online rooms) are optional. The free version shows a banner ad, and a one-time purchase removes it.

Playing without an account

Single-player games and multiplayer games on one phone work entirely on your phone. Your settings (such as vibration) and your best time are stored only on your device and are never sent to us. The free version's banner ad does connect to Google; see Ads and the Remove ads purchase.

What we collect when you sign in with Google

To appear on the online leaderboards or play in online rooms, you sign in with your Google account. (The app also has a sign-in with email, but only for accounts we create ourselves, such as the one Google Play's reviewers use; players can't sign up that way.) We then store:

  • Account details from Google: your email address, name and Google account identifier, as shared by Google when you sign in. We use them only to identify your account and let you sign in again on another device. We don't show them to other players.
  • Your profile: the nickname you choose, the number tag added to it (for example Name#1234), and the country you pick.
  • Your game times: for each single-player game you play while signed in, when it started and ended and the time you held on. Games in the other modes (moving and shrinking button) are kept the same way, separately. Multiplayer games on one phone are never uploaded, and neither are games played before you signed in.
  • Online rooms: the rooms you create or join, who is in each room, whether your button is pressed, how many rounds in a row you've sat out (2 in a row removes you from the room), when your phone last checked in (so players who lose connection can be removed), your time in each online round, how many rounds you've won in that room, and the latest emoji reaction you sent in it. A room is deleted when its last player leaves. Your online round times are kept for the multiplayer leaderboard.
  • Friends: your friend code and the list of players you've added as friends.
  • Daily challenge: for each daily try, when it started and ended, the time you held on, and how close you got to the day's target.
  • Challenges: the challenges you create (your verified best time when you made them) and, for each challenge, who beat it and with what time.
  • Friend activity: a record of your new all-time bests, the achievements you unlock, your best daily challenge result of each day and the challenges you beat, kept for 30 days so your friends can see them. Adding someone as a friend and inviting a friend to a room are kept the same way, shown only to that friend.
  • Stats and achievements: how many games you've played, your total time holding the button, how many games you've played with friends on one phone, the days you played (for your streak of days in a row), and the achievements you've earned, with when you earned them. Stats and achievements from before you signed in, kept on your phone until then, are added to your account when you sign in.

Notifications (daily reminder, friend news and a weekly recap) are optional and created on your phone. While they're on, the app checks our servers for new friends, room invites and your friends' news about every 15 minutes. While the app is open, it also keeps a connection to our servers so invites show up at once. We don't use a push service and don't store any device identifier.

To prevent abuse, we also keep a short-lived record of your recent attempts to add friends or join rooms by code, so they can be rate-limited; it's deleted automatically about an hour later. Single-player games that didn't set a record are deleted automatically after about a day.

Like any internet service, our servers also receive your IP address and basic technical details when the app connects. These are used to run and protect the service.

What other players can see

Your nickname with its tag, your country and your best times appear on the world, country and friends leaderboards, including the multiplayer leaderboard for online rounds and the daily challenge leaderboard (how close you got to the day's target). Your friends also see your activity: new all-time bests, achievements you unlock and your daily challenge results. Whoever made a challenge you beat sees your nickname and time. Anyone with one of your challenge links sees your nickname with its tag, your country and the time to beat, in the app and on our website. Signed-in players can also open your stats and achievements by tapping your name (on a leaderboard, in their friend list or in friend activity): games played, total time held, days in a row, daily challenge and online results, weekly bests and the achievements you've earned. In an online room, the other players in it see your nickname, tag and country, your button style and accessory, whether you're holding your button, your times as you play, and the emoji reactions you send. Your friends can invite you to their rooms. Anyone with a room's link or code can join that room, and anyone can join a public room through Quick match. Your email address and name from Google are never shown.

Ads and the Remove ads purchase

The free version shows a banner ad on the app's main screens (Play, Leaderboard and Profile), never during a game. The ads come from Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent ad fraud, AdMob collects information from your phone, such as its advertising ID, your IP address (which gives an approximate location) and how you interact with ads. Google explains how it uses this in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. If you're in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android's settings. We don't receive this information ourselves, only totals in AdMob's reports.

Remove ads is a one-time purchase through Google Play that turns the ads off for good. Google Play handles the payment, so we never see your payment details. The app asks Google Play whether your Google account owns the purchase and remembers the answer on your phone; it isn't sent to our servers. Once ads are removed, the app doesn't start the ad SDK at all.

What we don't do

  • No analytics trackers, and no ads other than the banner described above.
  • We don't sell your information, and apart from the ads described above, we don't share it with anyone for their own purposes.
  • We don't access your contacts, location, photos or other apps.

Where your data is stored

Online data is stored with Supabase, the database and sign-in provider that runs our servers, and sign-in is handled by Google. They process the data on our behalf. Ads are served by Google AdMob, and purchases are handled by Google Play. Data is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your online data until you delete your account. You can delete it at any time in the app: Settings → Delete online account. This immediately and permanently removes your profile, nickname, times (including online rounds and daily tries), stats, achievements, challenges, activity, friends, the rooms you're in and your sign-in account. You can also ask us to delete it by email; see how to delete your account. Deleted data may remain in our providers' encrypted backups for a short time until those backups are replaced.

Children

TouchIT's online features are not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
