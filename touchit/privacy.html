TouchIT Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

TouchIT is a game by BochoApps ("we"). This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and how you can delete it. You can play TouchIT without an account; the online leaderboard is optional.

Playing without an account

Single-player and multiplayer games work entirely on your phone. Your settings (such as vibration) and your best time are stored only on your device and are never sent to us.

What we collect when you sign in with Google

To appear on the online leaderboard you sign in with your Google account. We then store:

  • Account details from Google: your email address, name and Google account identifier, as shared by Google when you sign in. We use them only to identify your account and let you sign in again on another device. We don't show them to other players.
  • Your profile: the nickname you choose, the number tag added to it (for example Name#1234), and the country you pick.
  • Your game times: for each single-player game you play while signed in, when it started and ended and the time you held on. Multiplayer games are never uploaded, and neither are games played before you signed in.
  • Friends: your friend code and the list of players you've added as friends.

Like any internet service, our servers also receive your IP address and basic technical details when the app connects. These are used to run and protect the service.

What other players can see

Your nickname with its tag, your country and your best times appear on the world, country and friends leaderboards. Your email address and name from Google are never shown.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, and no advertising or analytics trackers.
  • We don't sell or share your information with anyone for their own purposes.
  • We don't access your contacts, location, photos or other apps.

Where your data is stored

Online data is stored with Supabase, the database and sign-in provider that runs our servers, and sign-in is handled by Google. They process the data on our behalf. Data is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your online data until you delete your account. You can delete it at any time in the app: Settings → Delete online account. This immediately and permanently removes your profile, nickname, times, friends and sign-in account. You can also ask us to delete it by email; see how to delete your account. Deleted data may remain in our providers' encrypted backups for a short time until those backups are replaced.

Children

TouchIT's online features are not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
